AI Quality Assurance · Retrieval-Augmented Systems

Every AI answer, scored before it reaches a customer.

A RAG evaluation platform that turns “does this answer look right?” into an objective, repeatable score — for every question your AI system answers.

The problem

Teams shipping AI chatbots and knowledge assistants have no consistent way to know whether an answer is actually correct, grounded in real source data, or just confident-sounding text.

Manual review doesn’t scale past a handful of test cases, is subjective from reviewer to reviewer, and rarely gets repeated after launch — so quality regressions go unnoticed until a customer complains.

What it does

The platform runs every question, answer, and set of source documents through four industry-standard RAGAS metrics, then produces a scored, shareable report — automatically, at any scale from one question to a full regression suite.

01 · RELEVANCY

Response Relevancy

Did the AI actually answer what was asked?

02 · FAITHFULNESS

Faithfulness

Is the answer grounded in real source data, or invented?

03 · PRECISION

Context Precision

Is the system retrieving the right information, without noise?

04 · RECALL

Context Recall

Is the system finding everything it needs to answer fully?

Excellent ≥ 0.85 Good ≥ 0.70 Moderate ≥ 0.50 Poor < 0.50

Why it matters

Catch hallucinations before customers do

Faithfulness scoring flags answers that sound right but aren’t backed by real source material.

No vendor lock-in

Works with OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, or Azure AI Foundry, and connects to any RAG API through a flexible, auto-detecting integration.

Two speeds, one framework

A point-and-click UI for ad-hoc spot checks, plus an automated batch/regression suite for CI pipelines — both feeding the same report.

Built on an open standard

Scores come from RAGAS, a peer-reviewed, widely adopted open-source evaluation framework — not a proprietary black box.

Reporting

Every run — from the UI or an automated batch — produces a professional, shareable report with inputs, scores, and quality labels for every test case, plus session-level averages. Results accumulate over time, so quality can be tracked across releases, not just checked once at launch.
